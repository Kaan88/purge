## Changes made while finishing M0

- **The rig hung after restore.** The guest clock jumps about 11 h after a restore, so the time-based process wait treated System and maintenance processes as "new". Every wait hit its 15-minute timeout. Waits now follow the installer's own PID tree (`Get-TreeProcs`). The rig self-test passes 49/49.
- **Live snapshots hang** with 3D acceleration on, so `rig-base` and `dirty-base` are offline snapshots.
- **Label includes.** `rigtool truth` label files take `"include": ["_os.json"]`.
- **Scorer.** An offered item that is absent from snap2 now scores `gone` (a no-op) rather than `danger`. Without this, every old UT/BCU item the vendor uninstaller had also removed counted as danger (51 for Python).
- **Relabels.** Python's MSI SourceHash and `pyshellext.amd64.dll` belong to the Python Launcher (kept_owner).

An independent audit of the rig and truth then found the following, all fixed:

- **List values.** A changed `;`-list whose segments are the same before and after (PATH with only a trailing
  `;` gone) is ambient by a built-in rule, not by per-app rules. A segment item whose preview was cut at the
  capture limit is "unknown", never "gone".
- **Depth-1 roots.** Values of a child of a depth-1 registry root were not captured, so their absence proved
  nothing; such items are now "unknown" instead of "gone".
- **Containers.** `PackagedCom` and `Explorer\FeatureUsage` were ambient as whole keys, which hid the package
  entries and the AUMID values apps put inside. Label rules can now be `split` (the container is ambient, its
  new children are judged one by one) or `first` (an attributable exception checked before the ambient rules).
- **Attributable OS traces.** Prefetch files of the app's own executables, jump lists whose AppID hash
  (CRC-64) resolves to the app, and new per-user UFH `SHC` slots are leftover, not ambient. 8 of 11 jump
  lists resolved.
- **Package store.** Raw deletes in WindowsApps, AppRepository, `Packages`, AppModel, Appx, AppContainer,
  CapAuthz, AppIso firewall rules and PackagedCom are deny. A package offer (Remove-AppxPackage) skips that
  check and covers the package's rows, including `AppContainer\Mappings` (by `Moniker`) and
  `PackagedCom\ClassIndex` (by its package child key). The `AppxAllUserStore` tombstones are ambient
  (report-only).
- **Macro recall** counts only apps with a non-empty truth.
